NOTES
01

Versions are gone. The rail is a reversible change log — "undo this change", never "roll back to v3".

02

Art direction and editorial guidance are now separate dimensions: Direction artistique holds palettes, matières and lumières; Narration, Thèmes, Valeurs and Objectifs hold the editorial line.

03

Confidence is a word (élevée / moyenne / faible), not a decimal. Only intensities that are genuinely comparative get a bar.

04

Discipline-specific detail (genres, BPM, tonalités) sits in its own wide card, so the same layout serves an illustrator or a filmmaker.

05

Universal patterns are the bridge to cross-sector opportunities, with rejected ones kept visible and dashed.
