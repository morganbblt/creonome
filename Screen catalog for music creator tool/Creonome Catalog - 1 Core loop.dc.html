Three quiet cards. Everything else earns its way onto the screen.

Static hi-fi catalog for a music-artist workspace (Nova Sainte — indie electronic, FR/EN). Light is the default; the header switch repaints every frame. Media areas are empty drop zones. Editing screens are gone from the MVP — every change happens in the chat sheet.

Progressive disclosure on Today No score breakdown anywhere No third-party content shown One editing surface: the chat sheet Horizontal nav, tinted buttons
01

Resting state is almost empty

A card at rest shows strategy, media, title, pitch and its action. Hook, why, effort and score appear on hover or focus.

02

A score, a word, a confidence

No factor bars, no weights, no arithmetic on screen. The reasoning is prose in the Why section.

03

We describe patterns, never replay them

Aggregates and prose only — no third-party thumbnails, handles or timecodes anywhere in the product.

04

One split button per card

Next step on the left, alternatives under the caret, pen icon for Modify. Price printed inside.

05

Chat is the editor

Script and storyboard have no editing screens. Staged changes, then memory scope, live in the right sheet.

06

Confirm once, or never again

Cost is stated before consent, and the user can switch off repeat confirmations for that action.

Four destinations in one horizontal bar. Operational things live behind the settings button.
